# Umeet design brief

Design read: for children (6-12) and parents living apart because of labor migration or distance; warm, safe, playful, never clinical.

Concept spine: living system / garden. The family's shared companion (pet or plant) is a garden the child and parent tend together; care makes it bloom, neglect or worry makes it wilt, and help heals it.

Delivery tier: editorial-plus (non-animated). Calm page with one interactive Tier-1 beat.

Animation mode: non-animated — user picked "Non-animated (recommended for you)" at intake (preferred photos over video).

Tier-1 technique: B2 Grade-shift interaction pair (wow-catalog B2). Two aligned renders of the same garden (wilted vs thriving). Desktop: a cursor spotlight brings the garden back to life where you point. Touch: the lit area grows as the section scrolls through the viewport. Reduced motion: static split state. Defense: the mechanic enacts the spine, care literally heals the garden.

Locked palette (user-approved brand kit "Grow Together", overrides defaults):
- Morning Mist #F4FBF6 (background)
- Deep Forest #1F3A34 (text, dark blocks)
- Sprout Green #4FB874 (primary accent, fills with Forest text for AA contrast)
- Mint #B5E8D3 (support panels)
- Peach #FFB38A, Sun #FFD966 (warm accents, moods)
Defense: the user's approved brand palette; light pristine theme, no banned families.

Locked type (user-approved): Fredoka 600 display, Nunito 400/700 body (Google Fonts). Brand-approved, overrides the recipe pairing table.

Logo: user-approved Pet U mascot SVG (public/assets/logo.svg); no generated replacement.

Combinatorial pick: Pristine Light; solid with soft ambient depth; expressive rounded display; hero = massive image-first with restrained stacked text; section system = asymmetric premium flow; signature components = gapless bento, layered image crop frames, product UI panel (real generated screens), split diptych; second-read moment = one material switch (Deep Forest diptych for parents).

Corner language: all-soft. Cards 24px, large panels 40px, pills only for chips.

Section plan (landing "/"):
1. Hero: stacked center headline + massive showcase image (image-first)
2. Widget: mint panel split, text left, bleeding widget closeup right, live mood picker
3. Grow together: full-bleed image-as-canvas garden (B2 spotlight) + overlaid text card with Pet/Plant switch
4. Care: asymmetric 4-cell bento (questions, strengths, sick pet + therapy with parent OK, parent-first results)
5. Parents: color-blocked diptych (Deep Forest text half + dashboard screen half), flip chips
6. Final CTA band: Sprout banner with peeking mascot and ticket CTA, footer
Families: 6 distinct. Eyebrows: 1 ("For parents"), budget 2.

App ("/app", "/family/$key"): real product with D1. Parent creates a family, shares the child link/code. Shared moods (home-screen widget preview), companion care (feed/play with coins/energy), child check-ins earn energy, flagged results make the companion sick and go to the parent first; parent allows a session; child requests it and the companion heals. Parent sends quick notes ("I'm close", "I think about you", "I'm always here for you"), sees location label shared by the child.

Asset plan: showcase (approved brand mockup), widget closeup, garden thriving + wilted pair, pet happy/sick, plant happy/sick stickers, 8-icon generated set, parent dashboard crop, logo SVG, head kit (favicon set, manifest), OG/cover via branding.

CTA inventory:
- Nav "Open the demo": text link with a wavy route underline that draws on hover.
- Hero "Open the demo": chunky Sprout tag with the pet sticker cutout that hops on hover (CTA embedded in image cutout).
- Widget "Open the demo": inline link whose arrow travels along a drawn curvy path.
- Parent chips: flip like material samples to "Sent" on press.
- Final "Create a family": Sun ticket with perforation; the stub tears away on hover.

Anti-convergence ledger: first build in this chat; axes derived from the garden material world (moss, clay pots, paper notes).
